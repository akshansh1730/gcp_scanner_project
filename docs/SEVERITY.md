# Severity rubric

| Severity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CRITICAL | Data or control is exposed to the internet or audit evidence is destroyed/disabled. Fix now. |
| HIGH | A single step lets an attacker escalate privilege, reach a host, or hide activity. |
| MEDIUM | Weakens defence in depth or increases blast radius; needs another weakness to exploit. |
| LOW | Reduces visibility or hardening; hygiene. |

Default severities per check are in `CHECKS.md`. Phase 6 adds scoring and ordering.
